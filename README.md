# Career Compass Pro

LANDING PAGE BUILD PROMPT

Student Digital Presence & Portfolio Service

Build a premium, lightweight, conversion-focused landing page for a student-focused digital presence and portfolio service.

This is a front-end-only marketing landing page.

Do NOT build a backend, database, authentication system, CMS, dashboard, API, payment integration, or complex application logic.

Use only:

HTML

CSS

Tailwind CSS if useful

Minimal vanilla JavaScript only where necessary for small interactions

The website must be extremely lightweight, fast, responsive and easy to maintain.

01. BRAND CONTEXT

This is a dedicated student-facing brand managed by Ashra Designs.

Ashra Designs should NOT be the hero or primary brand.

The student should experience this as an independent, trustworthy career-presence brand.

The positioning is:

A career companion for ambitious college students who want to build their professional presence while they are still in college.

The brand is NOT:

A placement consultancy

A coaching institute

A resume-writing agency

A generic portfolio-design service

A LinkedIn growth agency

A motivational career platform

An AI-generated portfolio service

The brand helps students connect the different pieces of their professional identity:

Positioning → LinkedIn → Content → Projects → Proof of Work → Resume → Portfolio → Digital Presence

Nothing should feel disconnected.

The core philosophy:

You focus on becoming good. We help you show it.

The emotional positioning:

You don't have to figure it all out alone.

The service should feel like a knowledgeable senior or career companion helping the student build the foundation of their professional identity.

The primary objective is to make a student understand:

Why they should start early

What this service actually builds

How everything connects

What they receive

Which plan is appropriate

How to get started

The visitor should never feel confused about:

What exactly am I buying?

SECTION 01 — HERO

The hero should immediately communicate the transformation.

Primary headline:

YOUR DEGREE ISN'T YOUR WHOLE PROFILE.

Supporting copy:

Build a professional presence that shows what you can actually do.

Secondary supporting message:

A personalised digital presence system for students — built around your field, your goals and your college life.

Primary CTA:

BUILD MY PRESENCE

Secondary CTA:

SEE WHAT'S INCLUDED

Do not overcrowd the hero.

Use the brand logo prominently but elegantly.

The hero should have strong typography and generous negative space.

A subtle visual representation of interconnected professional platforms can be used if it fits the design system.

Do NOT fill the hero with floating logos everywhere.

SECTION 02 — WHY DIGITAL PRESENCE MATTERS

Headline direction:

Your work shouldn't disappear after the assignment is submitted.

Explain the problem in a human way.

Students are increasingly building projects, participating in competitions, internships, clubs, freelance work and independent learning.

But much of that work remains invisible.

A resume tells someone what you claim to have done.

A digital presence gives them somewhere to actually explore it.

Explain that recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators and potential employers can discover candidates through:

LinkedIn

Search

Projects

GitHub

Behance

Case studies

Personal websites

Other relevant professional platforms

Do NOT make unsupported claims such as:

Every recruiter checks LinkedIn.

Instead communicate the broader truth:

When someone is evaluating you, your digital presence can give them more context than a single-page resume ever can.

Core message:

Don't just tell people what you can do. Give them something to see.

This section should feel educational rather than salesy.

SECTION 03 — WHY START EARLY

Headline:

The earlier you start, the more you have to show.

Explain the compounding effect of starting during college.

Use a simple progression:

1st Year
Explore → Learn → Build

2nd Year
Build → Document → Publish

3rd Year
Show → Refine → Get noticed

Final Year
Apply → Interview → Demonstrate

Make the point that the goal isn't to become a professional overnight.

The goal is to gradually accumulate:

Projects

Experience

Proof

Content

Connections

Case studies

A visible body of work

Core message:

You don't need your career figured out. You just need to start building it.

Keep this section visually simple.

Avoid turning it into a complicated timeline graphic.

SECTION 04 — PRIMARY CTA

Create a strong transition section.

Headline:

That's why your digital presence deserves to exist in your own name.

Supporting copy:

Tell us what you're studying, what you're interested in and where you want to go. We'll help position everything around your direction.

CTA:

Supporting microcopy:

Built around your goals. Your field. Your schedule.

This section should visually separate the educational portion of the page from the product offering.

SECTION 05 — YOUR DIGITAL TOOLKIT

Headline:

The right tools. Connected around you.

Explain that there is no universal platform stack.

Different careers require different proof.

For example:

Professional identity, networking and visibility.

GitHub

Code, technical projects and development work.

Behance

Design work and visual case studies.

Dribbble

Design exploration and creative work.

Notion

Organised knowledge, projects and documentation.

Personal Website

Your own professional home on the internet.

The important message:

You don't need all of them. You need the right ones for where you're going.

The platform selection should be based on:

Degree

Domain

Target role

Existing experience

Career direction

Type of work they want to showcase

Make this feel curated rather than overwhelming.

SECTION 06 — WHAT WE BUILD

Headline:

One presence. Everything connected.

Explain that the service isn't a collection of disconnected deliverables.

Everything is positioned around one professional identity.

Show a visual flow:

Your Goals

Positioning
↓
LinkedIn
↓
Content

Projects

Include concise explanations.

POSITIONING

LINKEDIN

Complete profile creation and optimisation based on their positioning.

30-DAY CONTENT

A personalised 30-day LinkedIn content system prepared around their niche and positioning.

Make clear that the content is ready to publish/schedule and aligned with the rest of their presence.

PROOF OF WORK

RESUME

An ATS-friendly resume written around their actual experience, expertise and career direction.

Do not promise guaranteed ATS scores or job outcomes.

2 PROJECTS

Two relevant portfolio projects developed with guidance and support.

These are designed around the student's target domain and become visible proof of capability.

Make clear that these are portfolio/demo projects, not fabricated professional employment experience.

08. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The design must be genuinely responsive.

Desktop:

Strong editorial layout

Wide negative space

Multi-column sections where appropriate

Clear visual rhythm

Tablet:

Reduce spacing

Maintain hierarchy

Mobile:

Single-column layout

Large but controlled typography

No horizontal overflow

Cards become stacked sections

CTAs become full-width where appropriate

Pricing comparison becomes vertically readable

Preserve whitespace

Do NOT simply shrink the desktop design.

Recompose the layout for mobile.

Floating objects

Optimised images

No unnecessary JavaScript

No backend

Semantic HTML

Accessible buttons and links

Supercharge your profile

Next-generation career platform

Empower yourself

Unleash your potential

Stand out from the crowd

Prefer:

You don't need your career figured out. You need somewhere to start.

Your work shouldn't disappear after the assignment is submitted.

Don't just say you're good at something. Give people something to see.

You focus on becoming good. We help you show it.

Built around college, not against it.

You don't need every platform. You need the right ones.

Your portfolio should grow as you do.

Keep the tone:

calm + intelligent + slightly youthful + trustworthy.

Not childish.

Not overly Gen Z.

COLLEGE-FRIENDLY

OWNERSHIP

16. IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE

The website should NOT feel like it is selling a list of services.

It should communicate one transformation:

From a student with scattered achievements and work → to a student with one credible, connected professional presence.

The services are simply the components that create that transformation.

The visitor should understand:

I study → I build → I document → I show → I grow.

This brand helps me connect those steps.

17. FINAL QUALITY BAR

Before considering the page complete, review it as if you were an ambitious 18–22 year-old student from an IIT, NIT, BITS, top private university or strong Tier 2 college.

Ask:

Do I immediately understand what this is?

Do I understand why I need it?

Do I understand why starting now matters?

Does this feel personalised?

Does this feel trustworthy?

Does this feel like it understands college life?

Do I understand exactly what ₹5,000 includes?

Do I understand exactly what ₹12,000 includes?

Do I understand the difference between the two?

Does the website feel premium enough to justify the price?

Does anything feel like a generic AI template?

Is there unnecessary visual clutter?

Can I scan the entire page quickly?

Does the CTA feel natural rather than desperate?

If any answer is no, refine the page.

Do not add more elements to solve a design problem. Remove or simplify first.

The final result should feel like a carefully art-directed student career brand with a strong point of view, not a generic landing-page template.

Use the supplied design.md as the final authority for all visual decisions.
